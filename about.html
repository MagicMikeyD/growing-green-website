Built by farmers, for farmers

Modern farm software shouldn't be a luxury.

Growing Green started because the tools available to small farms either felt built for industrial agriculture or felt built for hobbyists. Nothing in between. We're a small team focused on making the most powerful farm management software accessible to operations of any size — without the upcharges, hidden fees, or app-store middlemen.

The team

M
Mikey Floyd Founder, Mana Softworks LLC

Mikey founded Mana Softworks to build tools the farming community has been asking for. Growing Green is the first product — a single platform connecting farmers, customers, and workers with offline-first software that respects the way farms actually run.

Our mission

🌱

Empower farmers with tools

Modern software shouldn't be a luxury. Every farm deserves the same operating system as the largest operations.

🤝

Build community via co-ops

Farms that pool resources, share inventory, and bulk-purchase as a group can match the buying power of much larger operations.

📈

Reduce churn

Small farms close because the math doesn't work. Better tools, better visibility, better margins — make farming financially sustainable.

Where we're headed

Growing Green is shipping in expansion packs — small, focused releases that add depth without bloating the apps. The Livestock Pack is in beta now. Co-op tools are next, followed by Operations (field mapping, weather, equipment), Financials (P&L, tax exports), and a full marketplace with wholesale and B2B. AI assistance — pest detection, yield forecasting, smart irrigation — comes later, opt-in. We don't promise dates. We do promise a small, reasonable platform that grows with your farm.

Who we're built for

Small farms (under 50 acres) Replace the spreadsheet, the paper notebook, and the half-dozen disconnected apps you patch together every week.
Diversified operations Crops, livestock, value-added goods, and direct-to-consumer sales — all in one place.
Co-ops and CSAs Bulk buys, shared inventory, member management, and wholesale relationships.
