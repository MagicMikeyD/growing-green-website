Quick answers

How much does it cost?

$24.99/month for the Farmer App after the trial. The Market and Worker apps are free. Marketplace sales processed through Stripe carry a 4% royalty, capped at $400/month per farm. Cash sales are tracked but free.

What if I lose internet in the field?

The apps are offline-first by design. You keep working, your data stays on the device, and everything syncs automatically when you reconnect. No "waiting for connection" spinners.

Who owns my data?

You do. Always. You can export your data at any time, and if you delete your account it's purged from active storage within 30 days.
